The three states, live

The explainer's state diagram, driven by the recording. Every mobile is always in exactly one of three states; as the two minutes play, the five bodies sort themselves into the column they're in and slide across when they change.

At any moment, each mobile is in one of three possible states:

0

Searching

for a partner to engage in conversation.
0

Conversing

with a partner to satisfy their goals.
0

Resting

in order to recharge its “drives.”
